03 · Process

How a spun pipe is made.

From steel to site, on one line. Steps follow our plant list; the drawing is illustrative.

  1. Steel reinforcement is fabricated in-house.

  2. Concrete comes from our own batching plants.

  3. Automated spinning machines form each pipe.

  4. Pipes cure in our curing yards.

  5. Our testing laboratory runs compression, dimensional and load tests. Batches are certified.

  6. Pipes leave on our transport logistics: over 500 truck dispatches a month.
